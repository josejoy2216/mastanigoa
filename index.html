Places to Visit Near Mastani Goa

From quiet turtle beaches to dramatic Portuguese forts — all within 50 km of Korgaon, Pernem.

HERITAGE Local

Kamleshwar Temple, Korgaon

A nearly 200-year-old majestic temple right in Korgaon village — the spiritual heart of the area. A must-see starting point for your local exploration.

View on Map
BEACH ~5 km

Querim (Keri) Beach

The northernmost beach in Goa — pristine, uncrowded and incredibly serene. Accessible by a short ferry ride across the Terekhol River.

View on Map
FORT ~6 km

Fort Tiracol

A 17th-century Portuguese fort perched above the Terekhol river mouth. Stunning panoramic views of the sea and converted into a heritage hotel — open for day visits too.

View on Map
BEACH ~8 km

Arambol Beach

A beloved hippie haven with a sweet freshwater lake hidden behind the beach. Great for drum circles, yoga, paragliding and spontaneous jam sessions.

View on Map
BEACH ~10 km

Mandrem Beach

One of Goa's most peaceful beaches — powdery sand, calm waters and a river creek make it perfect for relaxation.

View on Map
BEACH ~12 km

Ashvem Beach

A long, clean and relatively uncrowded stretch with shallow waters safe for swimming. A short drive from Korgaon with great beach shacks.

View on Map
NATURE ~15 km

Morjim Beach

Famous as the nesting site of Olive Ridley sea turtles. The Chapora river estuary creates a stunning natural backdrop — great for birding and quiet walks.

View on Map
FORT ~30 km

Chapora Fort

Made iconic by Dil Chahta Hai — this ruined Portuguese fort offers breathtaking views of Vagator Beach and the Chapora river. Free entry.

View on Map
BEACH ~30 km

Vagator Beach

Home to dramatic red laterite cliffs, a tiny rocky cove and some of Goa's best beach clubs. North and Little Vagator each have their own distinct character.

View on Map
MARKET ~35 km

Anjuna Beach & Flea Market

The iconic Wednesday flea market draws hundreds of stalls with clothes, handicrafts and jewellery. The beach itself is rocky and atmospheric.

View on Map
MARKET ~35 km

Mapusa Friday Market

The busiest local market in North Goa — held every Friday. Best place to buy Goan sausages, cashew feni, spices and fresh produce.

View on Map
BEACH ~42 km

Calangute Beach

The 'Queen of Beaches' and the most popular stretch in North Goa. Buzzing with water sports, beach shacks and vibrant markets.

View on Map
NATURE ~45 km

Mayem Lake, Bicholim

A peaceful inland lake surrounded by green hills — great for pedal boating. Nearby Bicholim market is also famous for handmade pottery.

View on Map
FORT ~50 km

Fort Aguada

The most-visited fort in Goa — a 17th-century Portuguese sea fort with a four-storey lighthouse. The panoramic view of the Arabian Sea is unforgettable.

View on Map
